04 — A PRACTICAL METHOD

How to use a Kakobuy spreadsheet.

A useful list preserves the reason you saved an item. Keep enough detail to compare similar products without starting your research again.

Start browsing on CAFANS ↗

  1. Choose one category and a spending limit.

    Start with shoes, hoodies, T-shirts or accessories. Set a total budget that leaves room for delivery and applicable charges.

  2. Record the exact product information.

    Save the listing link, selected variant, measurements, listed price and the date you checked it. Note anything the listing does not explain.

  3. Compare evidence, fit and total cost.

    Read the size chart and return terms. Examine available detail photographs and distinguish seller descriptions from independently supported information.

  4. Recheck the destination before ordering.

    Availability, prices and delivery options can change. Confirm the final item, selected size and checkout amount with the service handling your purchase.

06 — QUICK ANSWERS

Before you
open another tab.

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize shopping links and comparison notes. Depending on its creator, it may include categories, prices, sizing details or reference photos. A listing is a starting point for checking a product, not proof of quality or authenticity.

Is this an official Kakobuy website?

No. This is an independent discovery guide that links to CAFANS. It does not represent Kakobuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès.

Does a spreadsheet price include international shipping?

Do not assume it does. Check the product price, domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping and any applicable taxes separately before ordering.

How should I compare clothing sizes?

Compare the seller’s measurements with a garment you already own. Check the measurement units, fit notes and any available measured photos rather than relying only on a size label.

Do these photographs verify the products on CAFANS?

No. The photographs illustrate fashion categories. They do not verify CAFANS inventory, seller relationships, authenticity or availability. Check the destination listing for the actual item and its supporting information.
